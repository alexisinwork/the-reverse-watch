# Research competitors

**Allowed sources:** public pages (features, prices, help centres, API documentation, terms), and a trial only after reading its terms and confirming they allow this use, with honest company details and synthetic data. Never use someone else's login, private customer accounts, scraping, decompiling, or anything that bypasses access controls.

**For each competitor, record:** product, link, date, plan and price, the claim, and confidence (_observed_, _vendor claim_ or _hypothesis_). A missing claim does not prove a missing feature.

**No screenshots, copied text or copied designs** in the repository. Write requirements and designs in your own words.

**Output:** a research note in `docs/research/` with a comparison table, where the product could be better, where it lacks, open questions, and links.

**Always answer:** why would a customer switch from this competitor? If there is no answer, say so plainly.
